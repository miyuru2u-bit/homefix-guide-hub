A property sale does not automatically transfer every service contract. The existing holder and buyer should follow the agreement's ownership-change procedure.

## Transfer checklist

1. Locate the transfer and cancellation clauses.
2. Confirm the property sale is an eligible transfer event.
3. Ask who must submit the request.
4. Record the deadline and required documents.
5. Confirm any fee and who pays it.
6. Verify the new contract holder and effective date.
7. Check whether coverage, limits, exclusions, or service fees change.
8. Record the remaining term and renewal price.
9. Obtain written confirmation before treating coverage as active.

## Transfer versus new plan

Compare the value of the remaining contract with a new buyer product. Consider remaining time, benefits, limits, effective dates, waiting periods, optional coverage, and total cost.

## Existing failures

Transfer does not necessarily make a known, prior, or detectable failure eligible. The buyer should still inspect the property and review disclosures.
