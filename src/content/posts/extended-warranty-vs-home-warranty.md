Extended service plans and home warranties solve related but different problems. Neither is automatically the better value.

## Side-by-side questions

| Factor | Extended service plan | Home warranty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Usually one identified product | Multiple listed appliances and/or systems |
| Term | Fixed or renewable, depending on plan | Often renewable, depending on contract |
| Eligibility | May be tied to purchase date | May be tied to property and effective date |
| Claim cost | Deductible or fee may apply | Service fee may apply |
| Limits | Product-value or contract limit | Item-level and aggregate limits may apply |
| Provider | Retailer, manufacturer, or administrator | Service-contract company or administrator |

## Compare total cost

Use the same time horizon for both options. Include premiums, service fees, deductibles, excluded labor or parts, payout limits, cancellation terms, and existing manufacturer or credit-card coverage.

## Avoid double coverage

Overlapping plans do not necessarily produce two payouts. One contract may be secondary or exclude costs paid elsewhere. Keep receipts, serial numbers, registrations, and complete terms.

## Neither replaces insurance

Property insurance, manufacturer warranties, extended service plans, and home warranties cover different events. Review each policy rather than assuming one substitutes for another.
