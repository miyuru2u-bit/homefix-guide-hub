A provider name cannot determine the best appliance warranty for every household. The useful comparison is between current contracts for your address.

## Seven factors to compare

1. **Covered components:** Confirm the refrigerator, laundry, cooking, and dishwasher components actually listed.
2. **Payout limits:** Compare each important appliance limit with local repair and replacement costs.
3. **Service fees:** Include the fee in every realistic claim scenario.
4. **Exclusions:** Review maintenance, installation, cosmetic, secondary-damage, and pre-existing-condition language.
5. **Contractor rules:** Verify authorization, scheduling, re-service, and independent-technician policies.
6. **Repair or replacement:** Read who decides and how cash-out amounts are calculated.
7. **Renewal cost:** Compare first-year promotions with the expected recurring price.

## How to compare value

List each appliance's age, condition, remaining manufacturer coverage, and likely repair cost. Calculate annual cost with no claims, one major claim, and several smaller claims. Do not assume that an included appliance means every part or failure is covered.

## Evaluating providers

Use the official materials linked above to obtain current quotes and sample agreements. Apply the same criteria to every provider. Avoid rankings that do not disclose dates, contract versions, locations, or scoring weights.

> Verify all prices, limits, exclusions, and availability directly before purchasing.
