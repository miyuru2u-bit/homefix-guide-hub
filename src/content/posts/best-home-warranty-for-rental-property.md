For landlords, service coordination and occupancy eligibility can matter as much as price.

## Rental-property checklist

- Rental, investment, and non-owner-occupied eligibility
- Single-family versus multi-unit restrictions
- Authorized tenant or property-manager contacts
- Scheduling, access, and approval procedures
- HVAC, water-heater, plumbing, electrical, and appliance limits
- Vacancy, misuse, maintenance, and tenant-damage exclusions
- Multi-property billing and transfer rules
- Renewal, cancellation, and sale-of-property terms

## Evaluate the workflow

Map who reports a failure, approves access, pays the service fee, communicates with the contractor, and receives claim decisions. Request written confirmation for any tenant-facing process.

Do not assume a warranty guarantees faster service or satisfies landlord habitability duties. Maintain an independent emergency-repair plan.

> Disclose the property's use and verify eligibility and terms before purchasing.
