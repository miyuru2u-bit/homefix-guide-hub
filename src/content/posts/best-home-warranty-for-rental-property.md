For landlords, a home warranty is less about peace of mind and more about predictable costs and fast repairs. A failed water heater or air conditioner in a tenant-occupied unit is both an expense and a tenant-relations emergency. A warranty turns unpredictable repair bills into a flat monthly cost and gives you a single number to call — which matters even more if you manage multiple units or own property out of state.

This guide ranks the best home warranties for rental properties and explains what landlords should look for.

## What landlords should look for

**Explicit rental/investment coverage.** Not all plans automatically cover non-owner-occupied homes. Confirm the provider covers rental properties and that you've disclosed it as a rental.

**Fast contractor dispatch.** With tenants involved, repair speed is critical. A large contractor network means quicker service and happier tenants.

**High enough caps.** As with any home, expensive systems (HVAC, water heater) need adequate caps so a major failure doesn't wipe out a year of premiums in one repair.

**Authorized contacts / tenant access.** The ability to let tenants request service directly saves you from being the middleman on every minor issue.

**Multi-property pricing.** If you own several units, ask about discounts for covering multiple properties.

> 💡 **Tip:** Set up an authorized contact so your tenant can call in a service request directly while you remain the policyholder. It dramatically reduces the back-and-forth on routine repairs and gets the technician out faster — a big win if you manage the property remotely.

## 1. American Home Shield — best overall for landlords

AHS clearly supports investment and rental properties and pairs that with high coverage caps and decades of experience. For a landlord, the high caps protect against the big-ticket failures (HVAC especially) that would otherwise be a major hit to cash flow. AHS operates in all 50 states and lets you tune your premium via the service fee — useful for budgeting across units. Pricing starts around $29.99/month for systems-only ShieldSilver.

## 2. Choice Home Warranty — best budget pick

Choice's large contractor network is its biggest advantage for landlords: faster technician assignment means quicker tenant repairs and less downtime. With a flat ~$85 service fee and plans from $46–$60/month, it's affordable to scale across multiple units. The Total plan (with AC) is the sensible choice for most rentals.

## 3. Liberty Home Guard — best for customization

If your rentals have varied equipment — some with pools, well pumps, or extra appliances — Liberty Home Guard's 40+ add-ons let you tailor each unit's coverage. Its strong claim-approval and customer-service reputation also means fewer headaches when a tenant needs a repair. Plans run roughly $49–$60/month.

## 4 & 5. Cinch and Select

Cinch Home Services suits older rental units thanks to its rust/corrosion coverage and forgiving pre-existing terms — handy for aging properties — though its higher service fee ($100–$150) is a consideration per claim. Select Home Warranty is the cheapest per unit ($35–$48/month) and works for landlords prioritizing low fixed costs, as long as you accept its lower coverage caps.

> ⚠️ **Watch out:** Always disclose that the property is a rental when you buy. Some providers price or limit coverage differently for non-owner-occupied homes, and failing to disclose it can give them grounds to deny a claim later. Get the rental coverage confirmed in writing.

## A note on taxes

Unlike a warranty on your primary residence, a home warranty premium on a rental property is generally a deductible business expense, which effectively lowers its net cost. Service fees you pay on rental repairs may also be deductible. Confirm the specifics with a tax professional, but this tax treatment can tip the math in favor of buying coverage for an investment property.

## How to choose

- **Multiple or high-value rentals:** American Home Shield for caps + scale.
- **Budget, fast repairs:** Choice Home Warranty.
- **Varied equipment across units:** Liberty Home Guard.
- **Older rental units:** Cinch.
- **Lowest fixed cost per door:** Select.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I get a home warranty for a rental property?**
Yes. Many major providers — including American Home Shield and Choice — offer coverage for rental and investment properties. You usually just specify that the property is a rental when you sign up, and some offer multi-property discounts.

**Is a home warranty worth it for landlords?**
Often yes. A warranty makes repair costs predictable, limits emergency out-of-pocket bills, and speeds up repairs for tenants — which helps with retention. It's especially valuable for landlords managing multiple units or properties remotely.

**What is the best home warranty for rental property?**
American Home Shield is a top pick for rentals thanks to high coverage caps and clear support for investment properties. Choice Home Warranty is a strong budget option with a large contractor network for fast tenant repairs.

**Can tenants file claims directly?**
Usually the landlord (policyholder) files or authorizes claims, but many providers let you add an authorized contact so tenants can request service directly. Confirm this option with your provider.

**Are home warranty costs tax-deductible for rental properties?**
Home warranty premiums for a rental are generally treated as a deductible business expense, unlike for a primary residence. Confirm with a tax professional for your specific situation.
