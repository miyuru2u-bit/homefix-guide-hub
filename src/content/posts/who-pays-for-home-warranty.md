A home warranty can be paid by the buyer, seller, or another agreed party—or omitted entirely. The important point is to document the obligation precisely.

## Terms to specify

1. Provider and exact product
2. Buyer-selected options or minimum coverage
3. Maximum contribution
4. Responsibility for excess cost
5. Payment method at closing
6. Effective date and waiting period
7. Recipient of the final agreement
8. Remedy if the selected plan is unavailable

## Payment does not equal control

Even when another party contributes, the future contract holder should inspect service fees, limits, exclusions, cancellation, renewal, and claim procedures. A contribution toward a weak or unsuitable plan may provide little practical value.

## Avoid assumptions

Do not assume a warranty is legally required, automatically paid through escrow, or an effective replacement for inspection and disclosures. Requirements and customs vary; consult the relevant closing and legal professionals.
