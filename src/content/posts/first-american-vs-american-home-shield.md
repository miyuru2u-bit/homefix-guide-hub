First American Home Warranty and American Home Shield both offer coverage for selected systems and appliances. Neither is automatically the better choice for every property.

## Comparison checklist

Compare equivalent plans for:

- Covered appliance and systems components
- Annual premium and service fees
- Per-item payout limits
- Number-of-claim and aggregate-benefit language
- Waiting periods and cancellation terms
- Maintenance and pre-existing-condition exclusions
- Contractor authorization and replacement or cash-out rules

## Real-estate plans

If coverage is connected to a property sale, request the specific buyer, seller, or real-estate agreement. Those terms may differ from direct-to-consumer plans. Confirm the coverage start date and who is responsible for optional upgrades.

## How to decide

Weight limits according to your property. Appliance-heavy households should inspect appliance caps and component exclusions. Homes with older HVAC or plumbing should focus on systems limits, access work, code upgrades, and refrigerant language.

> Confirm prices, availability, and terms directly with both providers before purchasing.
