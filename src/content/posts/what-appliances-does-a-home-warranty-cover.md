“Appliance coverage” is only a category label. The agreement determines which products, parts, and failures qualify.

## Appliances to check individually

- Primary and additional refrigerators
- Freezers and ice makers
- Ovens, ranges, and cooktops
- Dishwashers and disposals
- Built-in, over-the-range, and countertop microwaves
- Washers and dryers
- Trash compactors and specialty appliances

Do not infer coverage from a room or appliance category. A covered refrigerator may still have excluded ice, water, shelving, door, or sealed-system components.

## Contract checklist

1. Covered item and location
2. Covered parts or exclusionary definition
3. Eligible causes of failure
4. Effective date and waiting period
5. Service fee and repeat-visit rules
6. Per-item and aggregate limits
7. Maintenance and installation exclusions
8. Existing or detectable-condition language
9. Technician authorization
10. Repair, replacement, and cash-settlement terms
11. Delivery, installation, modification, and disposal costs

## Add-ons

Optional coverage is not automatically broader or better. Compare the add-on premium and service fee with its separate limits, exclusions, and effective date.
