Purchase price is only one part of ownership cost. A model that costs less initially can require more energy, consumables, service, or early replacement.

## Total-cost checklist

- Purchase and installation
- Estimated energy and water use
- Filters, cleaners, and other consumables
- Manufacturer maintenance requirements
- Parts availability and pricing
- Authorized and independent local service
- Access and repairability
- Warranty scope and duration
- Expected fit and replacement constraints

## Compare exact models

Use full model numbers, current energy labels, manuals, warranty documents, parts catalogs, recall records, and local service information. Do not treat a parent brand as proof that every model shares the same cost profile.

## Maintenance planning

Follow manufacturer instructions for filters, vents, airflow, cleaning, loading, detergents, water treatment, and installation. Consider whether required tasks are easy to perform and whether consumables remain available.
