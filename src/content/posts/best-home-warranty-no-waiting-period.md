“No waiting period” can describe several different situations: real-estate coverage, continuation from another contract, an inspection-based exception, or a promotion. It does not necessarily mean every item is eligible immediately.

## What to ask

- Exact coverage effective date and time
- Whether each item shares the same waiting period
- Documentation required for an exception
- Treatment of failures discovered before activation
- Real-estate versus direct-to-consumer terms
- Whether adding an option starts a new waiting period
- Cancellation and refund rules

## Get it in writing

Do not rely on a phone statement or advertisement. Save the agreement, quote, waiver confirmation, inspection, and proof of prior coverage. A waiver may remove a time requirement without covering an already known failure.

> Verify effective dates and exceptions directly with the provider before paying.
