The terms “home warranty,” “appliance warranty,” “extended warranty,” and “service contract” are sometimes used inconsistently. Start by identifying the seller, administrator, covered property, and governing agreement.

## Comparison checklist

| Factor | Questions to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Which products, systems, components, and causes are listed? |
| Effective dates | When does coverage start and end? |
| Cost | What are the premium, deductible, service fee, and other charges? |
| Service | Who authorizes and performs diagnosis and repair? |
| Limits | Are there per-item, per-claim, aggregate, or replacement limits? |
| Remedy | Who chooses repair, replacement, reimbursement, or cash settlement? |
| Exclusions | What do maintenance, installation, accidental damage, and existing-condition clauses exclude? |

## Existing protection

Check manufacturer, retailer, builder, credit-card, homeowners-insurance, and other service-plan documents. Coverage may overlap without increasing reimbursement.

## How to decide

Use the same time horizon and realistic failure scenarios for both options. Include delivery, installation, access, modifications, disposal, and downtime—not only the plan price.
