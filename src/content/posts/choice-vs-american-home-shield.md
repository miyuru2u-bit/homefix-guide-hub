Choice Home Warranty and American Home Shield both sell service contracts for home systems and appliances. A useful comparison starts with the agreements available at your address—not national starting prices or an unsupported overall winner.

## Comparison checklist

| Factor | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Price | Annual premium, service fees, renewal rate, promotions |
| Coverage | Exact items and components in equivalent plans |
| Limits | Per-item caps, aggregate limits, cash-out provisions |
| Exclusions | Maintenance, installation, pre-existing conditions, secondary damage |
| Service | Contractor authorization, scheduling, re-service rules |
| Cancellation | Waiting period, refund formula, administrative fees |

## How to choose

Choice may be worth considering if its current plan structure and service process fit your budget. AHS may be worth considering if its available tiers and service-fee choices better match your systems and appliances. Neither conclusion should be made without checking local contractor availability and the state-specific agreement.

Run three annual-cost scenarios: no claims, one major claim, and several smaller claims. Then compare the maximum benefit for the items most likely to fail.

> Plan names, prices, limits, and availability change. Confirm every material detail directly with both providers.
