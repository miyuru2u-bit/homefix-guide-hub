Affordability should be measured as expected total cost, not the lowest advertised monthly price.

## Total-cost formula

Calculate:

**annual premium + expected service fees + likely uncovered costs − realistic covered benefits**

Run at least three scenarios:

1. No claims
2. One moderate covered failure
3. One expensive or multiple smaller failures

## Cheap-plan risks

- Important appliances or systems omitted
- Low per-item limits
- Higher service fees
- Introductory price followed by a higher renewal
- Narrow component definitions
- Exclusions for access, modifications, maintenance, or existing conditions
- Cash-out below replacement cost

## Compare equivalent coverage

Request quotes on the same day and normalize annual versus monthly billing. Use the same covered items and service-fee assumptions. Record the quote date, promotion, renewal language, and state agreement.

> A lower premium is only valuable when the contract still covers the risks you intend to transfer.
