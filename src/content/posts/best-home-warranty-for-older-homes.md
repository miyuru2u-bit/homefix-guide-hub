Older properties can contain aging equipment, undocumented maintenance, obsolete parts, and systems that require code or access work. Those details should drive the comparison.

## Older-home checklist

- Definitions of detectable and pre-existing conditions
- Maintenance and documentation requirements
- Rust, corrosion, sediment, and improper-installation terms
- Limits for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and major appliances
- Access, permits, code upgrades, and modifications
- Obsolete-parts and cash-out provisions
- Waiting period and inspection requirements
- Contractor availability for older equipment

## Before buying

Document model numbers, approximate ages, known defects, and recent service. Do not buy coverage expecting it to pay for an existing failure. Compare the maximum benefit with the likely local cost of repairing or replacing each aging item.

> Verify all conditions and limits in the agreement issued for the property.
