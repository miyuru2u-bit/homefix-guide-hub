ShieldComplete and ShieldPlatinum are broader American Home Shield plan tiers. The useful question is not which plan “wins,” but whether the added premium buys meaningful protection for your property.

## What to compare

| Area | Contract questions |
| --- | --- |
| Price | What is the annual premium at the same service-fee level? |
| HVAC | Which components, refrigerant costs, and maximum benefits apply? |
| Appliances | What are the per-item limits and excluded components? |
| Roof | Which roof types, locations, causes, and limits qualify? |
| Code and access | Are permits, modifications, access, or code upgrades included? |
| Maintenance | What records or conditions can affect eligibility? |

## How to decide

Start with the annual price difference. Then assign value only to benefits you are likely to use. A roof benefit with a narrow limit or an HVAC benefit that excludes the expected failure may not justify the upgrade.

For older or expensive equipment, compare the higher-tier maximum benefit with a realistic local repair or replacement cost. For newer equipment still under manufacturer coverage, overlapping benefits may have less value.

> Confirm the current plan names, prices, benefits, limits, and state-specific agreement directly with American Home Shield.
