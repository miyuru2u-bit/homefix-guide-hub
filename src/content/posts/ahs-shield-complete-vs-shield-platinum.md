Complete = best value. Platinum = biggest safety net.

American Home Shield's two flagship plans — Shield Complete and Shield Platinum — cover the same list of items. The difference is *how much* AHS will pay when something big fails, plus a handful of Platinum-only extras. Here's the side-by-side, above the fold.

## What's actually different

Both plans cover the same 23 items — heating, cooling, ductwork, plumbing, electrical, water heater, refrigerator, oven/range, dishwasher, built-in microwave, washer, dryer, and more. The differences all live in the fine print:

- **Coverage caps.** Complete pays up to a standard per-item cap (often $2,000–$3,000 on HVAC). Platinum roughly doubles the ceiling on the highest-cost items. If your 15-year-old AC compressor dies, that gap can be the difference between a covered replacement and a $1,500 out-of-pocket bill.
- **HVAC tune-up.** Platinum includes one free annual maintenance visit — the kind of preventive service that keeps a claim from being denied later for "lack of maintenance."
- **Roof-leak coverage.** Platinum adds limited repair coverage for leaks over the primary living area. Complete does not.
- **Code and permit allowance.** When a repair triggers a building-code upgrade (a common issue in older homes), Platinum contributes toward the cost. Complete leaves that bill to you.

> 💡 **Tip:** The Platinum extras aren't flashy, but they target the exact scenarios where home warranty claims blow past coverage limits — old HVAC, city inspections, and roof intrusions. If any of those apply to your home, the math usually favors Platinum.

## Pricing and the service fee

Both plans use the same selectable service fee — roughly **$100 or $125 per visit**. Picking the higher fee lowers your monthly premium on either plan; picking the lower fee raises it. The gap between Complete and Platinum tends to stay around **$20–$30/month** at the same service-fee level.

One Platinum HVAC claim that hits the upgraded cap can pay back a full year of the price difference. That's the whole argument.

## Who should pick Shield Complete

- Newer home (built in the last ~10–15 years).
- Standard-brand appliances still under manufacturer warranty for the first year or two.
- HVAC less than 8 years old.
- You want the best balance of premium and coverage.

## Who should pick Shield Platinum

- Older HVAC or systems approaching end-of-life.
- High-end appliances that would blow past standard payout caps.
- Home in a city with strict permits and building-code enforcement.
- You want the free annual HVAC tune-up and roof-leak protection.

> ⚠️ **Watch out:** Neither plan covers pre-existing conditions, cosmetic damage, or failures caused by lack of maintenance. Documenting the working condition of your systems at signup — photos, service receipts — is the single best way to avoid a denied claim on either tier.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between AHS Shield Complete and Shield Platinum?**
Shield Complete covers all 23 major home systems and appliances at standard coverage caps. Shield Platinum adds higher payout caps (especially on HVAC and appliances), a free annual HVAC tune-up, roof-leak repair coverage, and an allowance for building-code upgrades and permits during a covered repair.

**Is Shield Platinum worth the extra cost over Shield Complete?**
Platinum is worth it if you have older HVAC equipment, high-end appliances, or live in an area with strict building codes — one big HVAC or code-related claim can easily eat the $20–$30/month upgrade cost for a year. For newer homes with standard appliances, Complete gives you the better value.

**How much does each plan cost?**
Pricing varies by ZIP code and your selected service fee, but Shield Complete typically runs $50–$75/month and Shield Platinum around $70–$95/month. Choosing a higher service fee (about $125 vs $100) lowers the monthly premium on either plan.

**Does Shield Platinum cover roof leaks?**
Yes. Shield Platinum includes limited roof-leak coverage for the primary living area, which is not included in Shield Complete or Shield Silver. Caps and exclusions apply — read your contract for the specific allowance.

**Do both plans include the HVAC tune-up?**
No. The free annual HVAC tune-up is a Shield Platinum exclusive. Shield Complete customers can request maintenance work but it's billed as a separate service visit.
