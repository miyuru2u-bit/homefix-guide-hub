HVAC failures can be expensive, making contract details more important than a provider ranking.

## HVAC checklist

- Heating and cooling listed separately
- Covered equipment types, capacities, and components
- Refrigerant, recovery, and leak-detection terms
- Ductwork, thermostats, and zone controls
- Access, permits, disposal, and code-upgrade costs
- Per-system and aggregate limits
- Maintenance documentation requirements
- Local contractor availability and emergency-service rules

## Compare realistic outcomes

For each plan, calculate the premium and service fee, then compare the maximum likely benefit for a compressor, heat exchanger, blower, or control failure. A low premium is not useful if the relevant component or associated labor is excluded.

> Obtain a current state agreement and written quote before purchasing HVAC coverage.
