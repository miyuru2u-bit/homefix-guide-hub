HVAC is the single most expensive system most homeowners will ever repair or replace. A new central air conditioner or furnace can run several thousand dollars, and even a major component failure (compressor, heat exchanger) is a four-figure bill. That makes HVAC coverage the highest-stakes part of any home warranty — and the area where coverage caps matter most.

This guide ranks the best home warranties for HVAC and explains what to verify before you buy, so you're not stuck with a low cap when your system fails in a heat wave.

## Why coverage caps are everything for HVAC

For most appliances, the difference between a $400 and a $700 cap is minor. For HVAC, caps can be the difference between a covered replacement and a multi-thousand-dollar surprise. When you compare HVAC coverage, the single most important number is the per-item payout cap for heating and air conditioning. A plan with a high cap (or one that covers replacement at fair market value) is worth far more than one with a low cap, even at a higher premium.

> 💡 **Tip:** Read the contract's HVAC section specifically. Look for: the per-item cap, whether ductwork is included, whether the plan covers the full system or just certain components, and any tonnage or SEER limits. These details vary widely and determine your real-world payout.

## 1. American Home Shield — best for HVAC

AHS is the strongest HVAC pick because of its high coverage caps on systems and its systems-first plan structure. Even the entry ShieldSilver plan covers 14 major systems including HVAC, and higher tiers add appliances plus extras like a free HVAC tune-up (ShieldPlatinum). AHS covers HVAC regardless of age and doesn't require maintenance records, which is valuable for older units. Pricing starts around $29.99/month for ShieldSilver. For a homeowner whose biggest worry is an aging air conditioner, AHS is the safest bet.

## 2. Liberty Home Guard — strong systems coverage + service

Liberty Home Guard's Systems Guard and Total Home Guard plans cover HVAC with competitive caps, and LHG pairs that with industry-leading claim-approval rates and customer satisfaction. Its 40+ add-ons also let you extend coverage to related items. Plans run roughly $49–$60/month. It's the best choice if you want strong HVAC coverage and a smooth claims experience.

## 3. Choice Home Warranty — budget pick (Total plan required)

Choice is affordable and has a large contractor network, but there's a crucial catch for HVAC: air conditioning is only covered on the Total plan, not the Basic plan. If you want AC coverage, you must choose Total (~$55/month). With a flat ~$85 service fee and moderate caps, Choice is a solid budget option for HVAC as long as you pick the right plan.

> ⚠️ **Watch out:** Don't assume "HVAC" means both heating and cooling are included. Several base plans (including Choice's Basic) cover heating but exclude air conditioning. Confirm AC is covered before buying, especially if you live in a hot climate.

## 4 & 5. Cinch and Select

Cinch Home Services covers HVAC with moderate caps and adds forgiving terms (rust/corrosion, some unknown pre-existing conditions) — useful for older systems — though its service fee is higher ($100–$150). Select Home Warranty is the cheapest, covering HVAC on its Gold and Platinum plans, but its lower caps make it a weaker choice if you're protecting an expensive or aging system.

## How to choose for HVAC

- **Expensive or aging system:** American Home Shield for the highest caps.
- **Want great service too:** Liberty Home Guard.
- **On a budget but need AC:** Choice Total plan.
- **Older system, unknown history:** Cinch.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a home warranty cover HVAC systems?**
Yes. Most home warranties cover heating and cooling systems, provided the breakdown is from normal wear and tear and the unit was properly maintained. Coverage is subject to per-item caps and exclusions in your contract.

**What is the best home warranty for HVAC?**
American Home Shield is the most common top pick for HVAC because of its high coverage caps and strong systems plans. Liberty Home Guard and Choice (Total plan) are also solid options.

**Does a home warranty cover AC replacement?**
Yes, if the unit fails from a covered cause and can't be repaired, most plans will cover replacement up to your contract's coverage cap. High caps matter most here, since full AC replacement is expensive.

**Is there a waiting period for HVAC coverage?**
Usually yes — most home warranties impose about a 30-day waiting period before HVAC claims can be filed, to prevent buying coverage only after a unit has already failed.

**Does a home warranty cover a pre-existing HVAC problem?**
No. Known pre-existing HVAC issues are excluded by nearly all providers. The system must be in working order when coverage begins.
