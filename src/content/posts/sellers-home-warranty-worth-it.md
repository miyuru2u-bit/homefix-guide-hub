Seller or listing coverage is not a substitute for disclosures, inspection, maintenance, or negotiated repairs. Its value depends on a specific contract and transaction.

## Seller checklist

- Provider and exact listing product
- Property and occupancy eligibility
- Coverage start and end dates
- Premium, service fee, and closing charge
- Covered systems, appliances, and components
- Existing-condition and maintenance exclusions
- Cancellation if the listing expires or the sale fails
- Buyer-plan conversion or replacement terms
- Responsibility for payment at closing

## Marketing the warranty

Describe only confirmed benefits in listing materials. Avoid promising immediate service, complete repair coverage, or protection from every post-closing dispute. Provide the buyer with the actual agreement.

## Known defects

A warranty does not remove disclosure obligations or make an existing defect eligible. Work with qualified local professionals on legal and transaction requirements.

> Confirm listing and buyer coverage directly with the provider and closing professionals.
