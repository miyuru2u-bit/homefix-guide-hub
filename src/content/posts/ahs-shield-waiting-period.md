A waiting period is the time between enrollment and eligibility for covered service. The controlling dates should appear in your agreement or declarations—not only in an advertisement or verbal explanation.

## Verify these dates

- Purchase and payment date
- Contract effective date
- First date eligible service can be requested
- Effective date for each add-on
- Renewal date and any coverage gap
- Effective date for a real-estate plan

## Real-estate and renewal terms

A buyer, seller, or transaction product may operate differently from a direct consumer plan. Renewal may preserve continuity, but a lapse, tier change, or new optional item can have separate rules. Obtain written confirmation for your exact situation.

## Early failures

Do not assume that removing a waiting period covers a known or already developing failure. Definitions of pre-existing and detectable conditions may still apply. Keep inspection reports, maintenance records, photographs, and the complete agreement.
