An appliance warranty can be a manufacturer warranty, retailer protection plan, third-party service contract, credit-card benefit, or another product. These arrangements do not share one universal coverage standard.

## Coverage questions

- Which appliance and serial number are covered?
- Which defects, breakdowns, and components qualify?
- Are diagnosis, labor, travel, and in-home service included?
- Is prior authorization required?
- Is there a deductible or service fee?
- Are repair attempts or repeat visits limited?
- What are the per-claim and aggregate limits?
- Who chooses the service provider?

## Common exclusions to inspect

Review the actual contract for accidental damage, cosmetic parts, consumables, misuse, commercial use, improper installation, maintenance, unauthorized repair, environmental damage, power events, rust, corrosion, pests, and problems existing before coverage.

## Repair and replacement

Check who decides the remedy and how value is calculated. “Replacement” may mean a comparable product, store credit, reimbursement, depreciated value, or a capped settlement. Delivery, installation, access, trim, modifications, permits, taxes, and disposal may be separate.

## Before purchasing

Compare the plan with manufacturer coverage and any retailer or credit-card benefit. Save the receipt, model and serial numbers, warranty, service contract, installation record, and cancellation terms.
