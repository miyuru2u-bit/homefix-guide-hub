A home warranty can be negotiated in a purchase transaction, but it is separate from the inspection, appraisal, homeowners insurance, and seller disclosures.

## Buyer checklist

1. Name the provider, product, and optional coverage.
2. State who pays and the maximum contribution.
3. Confirm how the charge appears in closing documents.
4. Obtain the sample agreement before accepting the plan.
5. Verify the covered property and buyer details.
6. Confirm the effective date and any waiting period.
7. Review exclusions, limits, service fees, and cancellation terms.
8. Save the order confirmation and final contract.

## Inspection versus warranty

A warranty should not replace a home inspection or negotiations over known defects. An existing or detectable issue may remain excluded even when coverage starts at closing.

## Provider selection

An agent, broker, title company, buyer, or seller may help coordinate an order, depending on local practice. Check for referral relationships and compare the proposed plan independently.

> Ask the closing professional and provider to confirm all payment and activation details in writing.
