A home warranty can shift some eligible appliance-repair risk, but it does not guarantee savings or complete replacement.

## Value equation

Compare:

**annual premium + expected service fees + likely uncovered costs**

with:

**realistic eligible benefits under the contract**

Run no-claim, moderate-repair, and major-failure scenarios. Use local estimates and the exact item limits.

## Coverage checklist

- Each appliance and component you care about
- Effective dates and waiting periods
- Per-item and aggregate limits
- Service fees and repeat-visit rules
- Existing-condition and maintenance exclusions
- Contractor authorization and local availability
- Repair, replacement, and cash-settlement provisions
- Delivery, installation, modification, and disposal costs

## Existing protection

Check manufacturer, builder, retailer, credit-card, and prior service-plan coverage. Paying twice does not necessarily produce additional reimbursement.

## Self-funding alternative

A dedicated repair fund offers flexibility and preserves unused money, but it leaves you responsible for the full cost and service coordination. Compare the fund balance with realistic repair risks rather than assuming either approach always wins.
