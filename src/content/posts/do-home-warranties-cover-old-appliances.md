Older appliances are not uniformly covered or excluded. Review the agreement issued for the property and plan.

## Eligibility checklist

- Is the exact appliance included?
- Does the agreement state an age limit?
- When did coverage and symptoms begin?
- How are existing or detectable conditions defined?
- What maintenance and installation requirements apply?
- Are the failed components covered?
- What limits apply to repair and replacement?
- What happens when parts are obsolete or unavailable?

## Document condition

Keep inspection findings, model and serial numbers, photographs, maintenance records, and prior repair invoices. Documentation can help establish timing, but it does not override exclusions or prove that a future claim will be approved.

## Repair versus settlement

For an older appliance, the administrator may consider parts availability, repair cost, contract limits, and settlement provisions. A benefit may be lower than the installed cost of a preferred replacement.

## Buying after symptoms begin

Do not assume a newly purchased plan will cover an already broken or deteriorating appliance. Review effective dates and existing-condition language before paying.
