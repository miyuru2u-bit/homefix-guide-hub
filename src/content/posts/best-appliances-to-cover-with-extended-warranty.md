The phrase “best appliances to cover” can be misleading. Two refrigerators or washers in the same category can have very different prices, parts support, service access, and warranty terms.

## Model-level checklist

Score each appliance on:

- Installed replacement cost
- Access and removal difficulty
- Parts availability and support life
- Local authorized service
- Manufacturer warranty and component-specific coverage
- Credit-card or retailer benefits
- Service-plan price and fees
- Covered parts and labor
- Limits and replacement method
- Your repair savings and risk tolerance

## Category questions

### Refrigeration

Check sealed-system definitions, compressor and labor terms, ice and water components, food-loss provisions, and local service.

### Laundry

Check motors, bearings, tubs, pumps, controls, stacked-unit access, and whether unstacking or reinstallation is included.

### Dishwashers

Check pumps, motors, controls, heating, leaks, racks, installation, and resulting-damage exclusions.

### Cooking appliances

Check controls, elements, igniters, induction modules, glass exclusions, built-in access, ventilation, and gas-service requirements.

### Microwaves and small appliances

Compare plan cost and restrictions with installed replacement cost. Built-in products can differ substantially from countertop models.

## Decision rule

Buy only when the written eligible benefit and service access justify the total price and restrictions. Skip categorical assumptions and sales-floor promises.
