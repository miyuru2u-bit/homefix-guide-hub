Select Home Warranty and Choice Home Warranty are often marketed to budget-conscious shoppers. The lower advertised premium is not necessarily the lower total cost.

## Comparison checklist

- First-year and renewal premium
- Service fee and when it applies
- Promotion conditions
- Roof-leak terms and limits
- HVAC and appliance caps
- Waiting period and cancellation fees
- Contractor authorization and local availability
- Repair, replacement, and cash-out language

## How to compare value

Calculate the annual cost under no-claim, one-claim, and multiple-claim scenarios. Then compare the maximum benefit for an expensive HVAC or appliance failure. A low premium can be offset by a limited payout; a higher premium can still be poor value if important components are excluded.

## How to decide

Select may be worth considering if its current promotional and roof terms remain useful after reading the limits. Choice may be worth considering if its current plan structure better matches the items you need covered. Neither should be selected solely from a marketing comparison table.

> Confirm current quotes, renewal pricing, coverage, and availability directly with both providers.
