Select Home Warranty markets appliance, systems, and combined service-contract plans. Promotions can make the first-year price look attractive, but renewal terms and payout limits matter more than the headline discount.

## Quick verdict

Select may be worth comparing for lower-cost coverage when its limits are adequate for the systems you care about. It is a weaker fit when an aging HVAC system or premium appliance could exceed the contract's maximum benefit.

## What to verify

- Current plan names and components
- First-year and renewal prices
- Service fee
- Promotion conditions
- Roof-leak eligibility and maximum benefit
- Appliance and HVAC payout limits
- Waiting period and cancellation rules
- Contractor authorization and cash-out terms

## Promotions and renewal price

Free months and introductory discounts can reduce the initial payment while obscuring the recurring cost. Compare the full first-year total, expected renewal price, and service fees under realistic claim scenarios.

## Roof-leak language

Limited roof-leak coverage is not equivalent to complete roof repair or replacement. Check covered roof types, leak locations, maintenance requirements, exclusions, and the maximum benefit.

## Coverage limits

A lower premium can be offset by a smaller payout on an expensive failure. Compare each important limit with local repair and replacement costs before choosing a plan.

## Who should consider it

Select may suit homeowners prioritizing a lower premium for moderate-cost covered items. Those with expensive aging systems should compare higher-limit alternatives and self-funding.
